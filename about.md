# DeathShake

Adds a customizable **camera shake effect** whenever your icon crashes in Geometry Dash., please join my youtube channel https://www.youtube.com/@MexballREAL

## Features
- **Shake:** Enable or disable the death shake effect.
- **Speed of Shake:** Adjust how fast the camera shake plays.
- **Shake Intensity:** Control how strongly the camera moves on crash.
- **Camera Rotation:** Toggle subtle camera tilt during the impact.
- **Practice Mode:** Choose whether the shake triggers in Practice Mode.
